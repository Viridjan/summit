Summit · five new enemies

Original pixel artwork, sized for an 8px block world. Behaviour-inspired adaptations from Metroid: Zero Mission and Castlevania.

Metroid · 2 hits

Ripper

A slow horizontal patrol. Turns at walls and at the end of its patrol range.

Metroid · 1 hit

Skree

Waits overhead, flashes a warning, then dives. Rests after landing and returns to its perch.

Castlevania · 1 hit

Bat

Warns before a committed swoop toward your last position. Perches again after the attack.

Castlevania · 3 hits

Skeleton

Guards platforms and throws bones in an arc. Your attack can swat the bones away.

Castlevania · 2 hits

Medusa Head

Crosses open space in a bounded sine wave. Terrain stops it; it cannot leave its room.

Six of each type are placed in the Hollow Knight world. Choose Adventure → World: Hollow Knight. Attack with A; hold a direction to aim the swing.
